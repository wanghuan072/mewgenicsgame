## About Mewgenics

**Mewgenics** is a roguelike-inspired **cat colony** adventure from **Edmund McMillen** (The Binding of Isaac, Super Meat Boy). You raise and fight with cats in a strange world: dungeon-style **runs**, **items**, **events**, and **boss-style** encounters, with strong emphasis on **breeding**, **traits**, and **team composition**. The tone blends dark humor, body horror, and arcade pacing—expect chaotic fights, weird loot, and runs that can swing hard based on RNG and build choices.

Official storefront and announcements live on Steam and the developer’s channels; **mewgenicsgame.org** is an **unofficial companion** that collects **guides**, **reference lists**, and **community wiki–style** notes to help you plan runs and breeding without digging through scattered threads.

---

## What makes the game stand out

- **Breeding & genetics:** Cats aren’t just skins—stats, traits, and synergies matter for how a run feels and how you push into harder content.
- **Run-based structure:** Short loops, high replay value, and frequent “one more run” moments as you learn enemy pools and item combinations.
- **Build variety:** Weapons, trinkets, consumables, and class-style identities encourage different routes each run.
- **Boss & mid-boss spikes:** Pacing is punctuated by serious fights that reward preparation and knowledge of patterns and counters.
- **Events & chaos:** Blessings, tragedies, weather, and odd encounters keep routes from feeling identical.

Exact numbers and unlock rules change with patches—always cross-check in-game tooltips and patch notes when something feels off.

## How to use this guide

1. **New to the game?** Start with **[Classes](https://mewgenicsgame.org/classes)** to pick a playstyle, then skim **[Guides](https://mewgenicsgame.org/guides)** for practical checklists.
2. **Planning breeding?** Use the **[Breeding Tool](https://mewgenicsgame.org/breeding-tool)** to think through pairings and outcomes before you commit cats in a save.
3. **Stuck on a fight or route?** Open **[Wiki](https://mewgenicsgame.org/wiki)** and jump to bosses, mini-bosses, enemies, items, events, or NPCs as needed.
4. **Mods & news:** Browse **[Mods](https://mewgenicsgame.org/mods)** for Nexus-style listings and **[News](https://mewgenicsgame.org/news)** for site updates and roundups.

Use **[Search](https://mewgenicsgame.org/search)** from the homepage search box (or open `/search` directly) to find a name across guides, classes, wiki lists, mods, and news in one place.

## Tips for getting the most from a run

- **Read the threat, not the hype:** If an item reads funny, assume it has a downside until you’ve tested it in a throwaway run.
- **Boss prep is team prep:** Before a hard gate, confirm healing, mobility, and DPS windows—not just raw damage numbers.
- **Write down what killed you:** One line in a note file (“Act X / room Y / died to Z”) speeds up learning faster than repeating the same mistake.
- **Use the wiki lists as a map:** You don’t need to memorize every enemy—bookmark **Enemies** and **Items** and search mid-run when needed.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this site official?**  
No. **mewgenicsgame.org** is a **fan/community companion**. Always trust the **official game**, **Steam/Epic pages**, and **developer posts** for release dates, pricing, and anti-cheat / mod policy.

**Why doesn’t a wiki entry match my build?**  
Community data can lag patches or differ by platform. Treat numbers and spawn tables as **approximate** until you verify in your version.

**Can I use mods safely?**  
Mods can improve QoL or break balance. Read each mod page, check Steam workshop or Nexus rules, and assume **achievements or online features** may be affected unless the mod author says otherwise.

**Where do I start if I’m brand new?**  
[Home](https://mewgenicsgame.org/) → [Classes](https://mewgenicsgame.org/classes) → [Guides](https://mewgenicsgame.org/guides) → [Wiki / Items](https://mewgenicsgame.org/wiki/items) when you start recognizing loot.

**How do I find one name quickly?**  
Use [Search](https://mewgenicsgame.org/search) with the enemy, item, or guide keyword.
